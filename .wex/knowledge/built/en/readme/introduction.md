`symfony-accounting-fr` brings French law to `symfony-accounting`: the plan comptable général and the association chart, the FEC (export, validation, import), the VAT returns CA3 and CA12, corporate tax, the data of forms 2033-A/B, the invoice mentions of the CGI and the Code de commerce, SIREN/SIRET/VAT number checks, and the bank files of La Banque Postale and Crédit Agricole.

A ledger whose country is `FR` uses it as soon as the bundle is registered.
